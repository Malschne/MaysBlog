Terms & Conditions (T&C)

For Commissioned Painting (Wedding Painting & Custom Painting Commissions)

1. Scope

These Terms & Conditions apply to all commissions between Maleen Schneider (hereinafter "the Artist") and her clients (hereinafter "Client") in the field of wedding painting and custom painting commissions, regardless of whether the inquiry is made via email, WhatsApp, or a submitted form.

2. Scope of Services

The Artist offers the following services:

  • Live Wedding Painting: Painting on-site during the wedding or event.
  • Commissioned Painting from Photo Reference: Creation of a painting in the Artist's studio with subsequent shipping.
  • Custom painting requests outside the wedding context, subject to separate agreement.

The exact scope of services (subject, format, technique, date) is documented individually in the written offer for each commission.

3. Formation of Contract

Inquiries are made via email (and, going forward, also via WhatsApp). A binding contract is only formed once the order form provided by the Artist has been signed and — if agreed — the deposit specified in Section 4 has been paid. Verbal agreements or those made via chat are non-binding until written confirmation of the order.

4. Prices and Payment Terms

The total price is stated in the individual offer and includes materials, working time, and, where applicable, travel and accommodation costs pursuant to Section 6.

A deposit of one third (1/3) of the total price is due upon conclusion of the contract to secure the booking. The remaining balance (2/3) is due on the day of the wedding or upon handover/delivery of the finished painting. In special circumstances, an individual payment arrangement may be agreed upon with the Artist. This is at the Artist's discretion and does not constitute an entitlement.

5. Travel Costs

If the commission location is outside the Artist's place of business, the following applies:

  • Travel costs (e.g. mileage, train or flight tickets) are shared equally between the Artist and the Client. The means of transport is chosen by the Artist; the resulting amount is stated as a fixed sum in the written offer in advance. No subsequent billing based on receipts will take place. The Artist selects the means of transport based on economic and practical considerations and will not favor unnecessarily costly options.
  • Accommodation costs are included in the total price as a flat rate of €150.00 per required overnight stay, independent of the above. An overnight stay is applied when travel reasonably requires it (e.g. from a travel time of 3 hours) or in the case of multi-day events.

The resulting travel and accommodation cost share is communicated in writing prior to conclusion of the contract and forms part of the total price pursuant to Section 4.

6. Cancellation by the Client

  • In the event of cancellation up to and including one month before the agreed date, the deposit paid (1/3 of the total price) remains with the Artist; any payments made beyond this amount will be refunded.
  • In the event of cancellation less than one month before the agreed date, the full agreed price (100%) becomes due, or remains with the Artist in full, as the date can no longer be allocated to other commissions.

7. Cancellation by the Artist

If the Artist must cancel a live painting appointment due to illness or an emergency, the Client is entitled to choose between:

  • full refund of all payments already made, or
  • subsequent creation of a painting based on photo/video material from the event, in place of the live painting, at the previously agreed price.

8. Revisions and Change Requests

Minor revisions (e.g. detail adjustments) are included free of charge up to the 7th revision within the scope of the original commission. More extensive change requests — in particular changing an entire color area or a significant part of the subject — are subject to a fee and will be quoted separately in advance.

9. Delivery Time

The painting is typically completed within a few days after the event, or within 4 weeks (acrylic paints) / 8 weeks (oil paints) of receiving the photo reference. The anticipated delivery date is documented in the offer.

Unless personal handover has been agreed, the finished painting will be shipped at the Client's expense via insured shipping (e.g. Austrian Post, DHL). Shipping costs will be communicated prior to shipment and are payable separately from the total price. For damage occurring during shipping, the respective shipping provider is liable within the scope of its insurance.

10. Usage and Copyright

Upon full payment, the Client receives the finished painting as well as the right to use it for private purposes (e.g. personal reproduction for private use, sharing on social media).

The Artist retains copyright ownership of the work regardless (§ 1 ff. Austrian Copyright Act) and may use the painting or reproductions of it for her own purposes (portfolio, website, social media, advertising). Where identifiable persons (e.g. the couple, guests) are depicted, this is done solely on the basis of the separately signed consent form.

11. Warranty and Liability

The Artist is liable for the professional and order-compliant execution of the painting. As this is an individual work of art, there is no entitlement to photorealistic reproduction; artistic interpretation is part of the service provided.

For damage occurring during live painting at the event location (e.g. caused by paint, easel), the Artist is liable within the scope of statutory provisions and/or her general liability insurance, where applicable. Liability for slight negligence is excluded to the extent permitted by law.

12. Applicable Law and Jurisdiction

Austrian law applies, excluding the UN Convention on Contracts for the International Sale of Goods (CISG). For contracts with consumers habitually resident in another EU member state, the mandatory consumer protection provisions of that state remain unaffected.

To the extent legally permissible, the place of jurisdiction is agreed to be the Artist's place of business in Austria.

13. Final Provisions

Should individual provisions of these Terms & Conditions be or become invalid, the validity of the remaining provisions shall remain unaffected. Amendments and additions to the contract require written form (email is sufficient).
